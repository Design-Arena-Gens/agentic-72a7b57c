# 🚀 Quick Start Guide

## Access the Application

**Live URL**: https://agentic-72a7b57c.vercel.app

## 5-Minute Quick Start

### Step 1: Open the Website (30 seconds)
Visit: https://agentic-72a7b57c.vercel.app

### Step 2: Select a Category (30 seconds)
Click on any category card, for example:
- 📱 Electronics
- 🏠 Home Appliances
- 👕 Fashion & Apparel

### Step 3: Choose a Subcategory (30 seconds)
Click on a subcategory within your chosen category, for example:
- Television
- Mobile Phones
- Refrigerators

### Step 4: View Analytics (2 minutes)
The dashboard will load showing:
- **Statistics**: Total products, average price, price ranges
- **Charts**: Brand distribution, price distribution
- **Keywords**: Most frequent keywords in titles
- **Rankings**: Top performing titles

### Step 5: Explore Products (1 minute)
Scroll down to see the product list with:
- Product images
- Prices and discounts
- Ratings and reviews
- View/sales statistics

### Step 6: Use Features (1 minute)
Try these quick actions:
- **Filter**: Click "فیلتر" to filter by price, brand, or rating
- **Sort**: Change sorting (popularity, price, date, rating)
- **Copy**: Click "کپی اطلاعات" to copy product info
- **Export**: Click "خروجی" → Excel/CSV/JSON

## Common Use Cases

### For Sellers: Finding Best-Selling Products
1. Select your product category
2. Sort by "پرفروش‌ترین" (Best Sellers)
3. Note the common keywords in top titles
4. Check price ranges of successful products

### For Researchers: Market Analysis
1. Choose a category to analyze
2. Check the "برندهای برتر" (Top Brands) chart
3. Review "توزیع قیمت" (Price Distribution)
4. Export data for further analysis

### For Content Creators: Product Reviews
1. Browse to your review category
2. Filter by rating (4+ stars)
3. Copy product information
4. Download product images

1. ✅ Use a modern browser (Chrome, Firefox, Edge)
2. ✅ Stable internet connection recommended
3. ✅ Allow pop-ups for downloads
4. ✅ Grant clipboard permission for copy feature
5. ✅ Wait for data to load (may take 2-5 seconds)

## Troubleshooting

**Problem**: Page not loading
- **Solution**: Refresh the page (F5) or check internet connection

**Problem**: No products showing
- **Solution**: Try a different category or subcategory

**Problem**: Export not downloading
- **Solution**: Check browser download settings and pop-up blocker

**Problem**: Charts not visible
- **Solution**: Zoom out (Ctrl + -) or use a larger screen
